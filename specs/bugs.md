| BUG-029 | 153 / mobile workspace | On Android Chrome (Xiaomi) and in DevTools mobile emulation the `/m` chat screen collapses to its app bar: the tab bar and composer disappear and the rest is blank. Cause: the 1.12.0 home watchlist strip renders `SignedChange` with an `sr-only` (absolute) label; no ancestor was positioned, so the labels escaped `main`'s overflow clipping and widened the document (858 px on a 431 px screen). Android Chrome then grows the layout viewport (`innerHeight` 873 → 2184), and `useKeyboardInset` read `innerHeight − visualViewport.height` as an open keyboard (1324 px), hid the tab bar and shrank the frame to 0. iPhone Safari does not grow the viewport; local accounts without followed symbols do not overflow. Fix: `main` and the strip are `relative`; the keyboard inset counts only while a text field has focus, measures on screen (× `visualViewport.scale`) and uses `documentElement.clientHeight`. | Android phone or DevTools mobile emulation, an account with several followed symbols, open `/m` | Full screen with tab bar and composer | Only the app bar; blank below (owner report and production measurement 2026-11-13) | Fixed in 1.12.2 |
