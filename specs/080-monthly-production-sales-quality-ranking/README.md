## Feature
Monthly Production & Sales Quality Ranking

## Persian Title

## Files

- `agent-prompt.md` — ready-to-use prompt for coding agent

## Summary

This feature adds a deterministic and explainable ranking layer over existing monthly production/sales data. It ranks companies by the quality of their monthly report, not merely by raw sales growth.

Main outputs:
- top/bottom monthly production-sales reports
- market-wide ranking
- industry ranking
- quality score
- confidence score
- explanation drivers
- AI natural-language support in Persian
