# Feature 152 — TahlilApp Max subscription

**Status:** specification only; linked to [Feature 139's authoritative plan contract](../139-tahlilapp-session-exchange/TahlilAppIntegrationContract.md) and shared lifecycle tasks T-40–T-43. Separate from [Feature 151 top-ups](../151-tahlilapp-paid-credit-top-ups/spec.md), which can ship independently. **Max** is the confirmed customer-facing name. Proposed external slug: `sapio_max`, pending TahlilApp verification. TahlilApp supplies expiration; SapioAI never computes it from the current 90-day commercial term.

## Verified compatibility review

- **External mapping:** `TahlilAppOptions.PlanMap` and its validator permit only Free/Plus/Pro; `TahlilAppExchangeParser.TryReadPlans` explicitly accepts only `sapio_plus`/`sapio_pro`; `TahlilAppSubscriptionPolicy.Decide` chooses Pro over Plus; `ExternalSubscriptionSynchronizer` and `SubscriptionCreditLifecycleService.EnsureExternalPlanPeriodsAsync` persist only these paid plans and calculate Free fallback/cooldown. See `src/backend/FinancialCopilot.Infrastructure/Authentication/TahlilApp/TahlilAppOptions.cs`, `src/backend/FinancialCopilot.Infrastructure/Authentication/TahlilApp/TahlilAppExchangeParser.cs`, `src/backend/FinancialCopilot.Infrastructure/Authentication/TahlilApp/TahlilAppStatusParser.cs`, `src/backend/FinancialCopilot.Domain/Identity/TahlilApp/TahlilAppSubscriptionPolicy.cs`, `src/backend/FinancialCopilot.Infrastructure/Billing/Persistence/ExternalSubscriptionSynchronizer.cs`, and `src/backend/FinancialCopilot.Infrastructure/Billing/Persistence/SubscriptionCreditLifecycleService.cs`. That service currently creates 30-day credit cycles and Free fallback grants; neither matches the confirmed full-window/Free-once rules.
- **Plan and capabilities:** `BillingEntityConfigurations` seeds `SubscriptionPlanRow` with finite numeric `IncludedCredits` and separate capability rows. Plus and Pro capability lists **differ** (for example Plus has `AiQuery.DeepResearch`, and their `Watchlist.Symbols` limits differ). Unlimited cannot inherit Pro permissions by name; Product must approve an explicit capability/limit matrix. `PlanCapabilityService.ReadEffectivePlanCodeAsync` independently chooses Pro/Plus/Free from active external rows and checks capability limits; its current external-row read does not filter `IsRevoked`, which must be corrected for authoritative revocation.
- **Admission and accounting:** `AiFacadeBillingHook.TryReserveAsync` and `GetReservationCreditsAsync` enforce numeric sufficiency, then `UsageReservationAuthorizationService.ReserveAsync` checks wallet capacity, writes a numeric reservation and increases `ReservedAmount`. `EnsureReservedCapacityAsync` may increase it. `UsageFinalizationService.CommitAsync` debits wallet and writes a charge ledger entry; `ReleaseAsync` frees the hold. `UsageRefundService.RefundAsync` adds credits to the wallet. `UsageReservationRow` has no quota mode or source subscription ID, and `UsageReservation` rejects `reservedCredits <= 0`, so its domain invariant must become mode-aware. These are concrete change points; a display-only Unlimited flag would still fail at zero balance or create false debits.
- **Reads/UI:** `UsageController.GetMyUsage` returns numeric balance/reserved/available/included credits; `src/frontend/src/lib/market-view.functions.ts`, `src/frontend/src/lib/credit-progress.ts` and `src/frontend/src/components/app/sidebar.tsx` assume finite credit progress. Admin numeric credit history and reports must continue to show actual finite wallet/ledger numbers.

## Shared lifecycle dependency and entitlement model

Feature 139 T-40–T-43 owns this prerequisite for both features: Free receives **250 credits once per user**, valid only until its assigned expiration; Plus receives **260 total** and Pro **2600 total** per entire authoritative paid window. No monthly allocation, refill or reset. A genuine paid renewal creates one new keyed full-window grant; exhausted/expired Free never refills, including after paid expiry or cooldown. Historical 25-credit Free grants count as already received and do not trigger an automatic 225-credit supplement. Existing wallet/source rows need conservative reconciliation. Feature 152 does not duplicate that implementation. TahlilApp commercial prices are Plus 1,000,000, Pro 5,000,000 and Max 25,000,000 TOMAN per 90 days; these do not enter SapioAI's top-up webhook or determine entitlement dates.

Add canonical `Max` subscription plan with explicit `CreditQuotaMode = Finite | Unlimited` (or equivalent enum) on plan/entitlement projections. `IncludedCredits` may remain numeric zero for schema compatibility **only alongside the explicit mode**; zero does not mean unlimited. Wallet `Balance`, `ReservedAmount`, ledger numeric credits, reports and estimates remain finite numbers. No `-1`, huge grant, repeated grant or ambiguous null. Feature 139's official `subscription.plans[]` still carries `slug`, `starts_at`, `expires_at`; add the verified Max slug (proposed `sapio_max`) to configurable parser/map/validator and persist an `ExternalPlanSubscriptionRow`. No new upstream date or hardcoded duration is required. Effective unlimited access derives from an active, unrevoked Max window with `starts_at <= now < expires_at`, regardless of delayed expiry cleanup. When no such window remains, use another active paid plan if any, otherwise finite Free access **without a new Free grant** and subject to existing paid-expiry cooldown. Include Max in last-paid-expiry/cooldown/revocation logic. Renewal is a new authoritative window; no unrelated finite balance is reset, and expired finite/top-up sources do not revive.

**Proposed precedence when windows overlap:** Max > Pro > Plus > Free. This needs Product confirmation because the official multi-plan contract permits overlap. Each source window remains independently stored and revocable; effective plan selection must be consistent in `TahlilAppSubscriptionPolicy`, `SubscriptionCreditLifecycleService`, `PlanCapabilityService`, API reads and frontend. Max grants **no numeric allowance**. Existing finite/top-up sources keep their own remaining amount and authoritative expiry; entering or leaving Max does not erase unrelated valid finite credits. Feature 139's Free-credit forfeiture and paid-expiry cooldown policy apply to Max as a paid plan, but cooldown completion never creates a second Free grant.

## Request lifecycle

At admission, enforce authentication, account enabled state, explicit operation capability/limits and existing operational/rate controls. Revalidate entitlement as currently done. If an active Max window is selected, bypass numeric wallet sufficiency and create an idempotent reservation with `BillingQuotaMode=Unlimited`, source `ExternalPlanSubscriptionRow.Id`, `ReservedCredits=0`, and no wallet hold. A zero-credit reservation is valid **only** with explicit Unlimited mode/source. Store mode/source immutably in the reservation and carry them through `BillingReservationHandle` and charge ledger metadata. Duplicate request keys must compare mode and source as well as account/operation/fingerprint; never reinterpret a replay using the current plan. A finite admission keeps the existing reserve path and source holds required by Feature 151 when enabled. Read-side spendable finite balance excludes expired sources even when cleanup is late (Feature 139 T-42).

An Unlimited capacity upgrade is a no-op for wallet holds but still records the execution-cost estimate. On completion, record actual token usage and available provider-reported or estimated cost, operation, outcome and correlation as now, with `CreditsCharged=0`, `BillingQuotaMode=Unlimited`, and original source window; do not debit wallet or create a fictitious credit grant. Commit reservation and zero-credit usage entry atomically and idempotently. Cancellation, failure or timeout releases/finishes the reservation without a wallet change; retry returns the original result. A positive-credit usage-refund request for an Unlimited zero-credit charge is rejected as invalid; no refund ledger credit or wallet increment occurs. The original zero-credit usage audit remains. Existing finite usage refunds remain finite and source-aware.

**Crossing a boundary:** natural expiry or a different plan after an admitted request does not reprice/reclassify that reservation. It may finalize under its original Unlimited mode with zero debit even after `expires_at`, subject to the existing reservation lifetime; new reservations after expiry are finite/Free and must pass their own rules, without a new Free grant. Authoritative revocation follows Feature 139's current release-pending-reservations behavior: release outstanding Unlimited reservations, reject late finalization of those released reservations, and retain already committed usage audit. The same rule applies to a finite→Max transition: an existing finite reservation completes or releases under its finite hold; it is never silently made free. Replay of a committed request remains idempotent across plan changes.

## API and presentation

Extend `GET /api/v1/usage/me` and equivalent user/admin subscription summaries with `creditQuotaMode: "Finite" | "Unlimited"` and authoritative `subscriptionExpiresAtUtc` for the **currently effective window**. Keep `balance`, `reservedCredits`, `availableSpendingCapacity`, `planIncludedCredits` numeric/finite or documented nullable where already allowed; they describe finite wallet state, **not** Max spending capacity. Add quota mode/source to usage metadata and internal ledger/report projections so zero-credit Max operations are distinguishable from free/failed finite operations. Existing numeric report totals remain numeric. Frontend shows **«نامحدود»** with the expiry and hides finite progress for active Max; it never sends `∞` as a numeric value. On expiry/revocation it reverts to the effective finite/Free display immediately after refreshed entitlement, without waiting for cleanup.

## Rollout and decisions

Deploy parser, persistence, billing mode and UI changes together behind a feature flag; do not accept an upstream Max plan until admission and settlement honor it. Test active zero-balance use, expiry, revocation, overlap, renewal, mode-bound retries and all finite regressions on PostgreSQL. Feature 151's confirmed rule forbids **new** top-up checkout while Max is active, but an already paid Plus/Pro purchase retains its original window.

Remaining details: TahlilApp must verify the proposed Max slug; Product must approve the explicit Max capability/limit matrix and overlap precedence. The Free assigned-expiration source is a separate shared Feature 139 contract detail. The 90-day commercial duration never becomes a SapioAI expiry calculation.
