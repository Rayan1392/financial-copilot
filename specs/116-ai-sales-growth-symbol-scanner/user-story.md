# Feature 116 — AI Sales-Growth Symbol Scanner

## Status

`[ ]` Not yet implemented

## Story

As a capital-market user,

I want to ask the AI for a list of symbols whose latest monthly sales have grown against a requested comparison baseline,

so that I can discover companies with improving sales without knowing metric codes, constructing filters, or using fixed command wording.

## Business Context

The product already has reusable foundations for:

- natural-language scanner parsing and validation (`007`);
- deterministic scanner execution and structured result tables (`008`);
- explainable results (`009`);
- derived monthly-sales growth metrics (`006`, `026`, `057`, and `071`);
- governed metric aliases and comparison-period semantics (`015`, `072`, `073`, and `074`);
- monthly-sales point, average, and trend retrieval (`069`, `070`, `076`, and `077`).

Those foundations do not yet define an end-to-end governed use case whose primary objective is to identify and list all eligible symbols with positive or threshold-qualified sales growth. This feature adds that missing scanner capability without introducing ad-hoc SQL, LLM calculations, or provider-specific intent logic.

## Goals

The system must understand that the user is requesting a **list of symbols with sales growth**, even when the wording is not an exact predefined phrase.

The user may specify:

- only that sales must have grown;
- a minimum growth percentage;
- a minimum current-to-baseline multiple;
- the comparison baseline;
- sorting or result-count preferences.

Representative Persian questions include:

- `لیست سهم‌هایی را بده که رشد فروش داشته‌اند`
- `سهم‌هایی که فروششان رشد کرده را نشان بده`
- `کدام نمادها افزایش فروش داشته‌اند؟`
- `لیست نمادهای با رشد فروش بالای ۳۰ درصد نسبت به سال گذشته`
- `سهم‌هایی که فروششان نسبت به دوره قبل بیشتر از ۲۰ درصد رشد کرده`
- `نمادهای با رشد فروش بالای ۵۰ درصد نسبت به دوره مشابه سال قبل`
- `لیست سهم‌هایی که فروش این ماهشان نسبت به ماه قبل رشد کرده`
- `نمادهایی که فروششان حداقل ۲ برابر ماه مشابه سال قبل شده`
- `شرکت‌هایی که فروش ماهانه‌شان ۱.۵ برابر میانگین ۱۲ ماهه است`

Equivalent English and mixed-language questions must be supported where the existing semantic layer supports them.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Detecting list/scanner intent for sales-growth discovery.
- Resolving a comparison baseline from natural language.
- Supporting positive-growth, percentage-threshold, and multiple-threshold conditions.
- Executing the query over governed monthly-sales data.
- Returning a paginated structured result table.
- Explaining the interpreted comparison, threshold, periods, values, and data freshness.
- Supporting the same AI facade from web and Telegram adapters through their existing rendering contracts.

### Out of Scope

- Predicting future sales.
- Generating investment recommendations or buy/sell signals.
- Product-level sales growth unless separately requested by a future feature.
- Arbitrary formulas generated by the LLM.
- Comparing cumulative YTD values unless the user explicitly requests a future supported YTD scanner metric.
- Treating quarterly financial-statement revenue as monthly production/sales data.

## Dedicated Use Case

Introduce a governed scanner use case or semantic specialization:

`SalesGrowthSymbolScanner`

This is executed through the existing `POST /api/ai/v1/query` orchestration and scanner boundary. It must not expose a separate public natural-language parsing endpoint.

The implementation may reuse the generic scanner plan and engine, but the resulting plan must make the following semantics explicit and validated:

```text
Metric family: MonthlySales
Objective: ListMatchingSymbols
Current observation: Latest eligible complete monthly sales period
Comparison baseline: PreviousMonth | SameMonthPreviousYear | AveragePrevious12Months
Threshold kind: Positive | Percent | Multiple
Threshold value: optional decimal
Sort: default GrowthPercent descending
```

## Intent Detection and Language Understanding

### Required Classification

The orchestrator must classify a question as this scanner use case when all of the following are present, explicitly or semantically:

1. A plural/list/discovery objective over companies, shares, or symbols.
2. Monthly sales or the product's governed sales concept.
3. Increase, growth, improvement, exceeding, or becoming a multiple of a baseline.

The system must not depend on exact text matching. The LLM may propose the intent and structured parameters, but backend validation and the governed alias/period registry establish the executable meaning.

### Examples That Must Route Here

- `چه سهم‌هایی رشد فروش داشتند؟`
- `فهرست شرکت‌هایی که درآمد فروششان بهتر شده`
- `نمادهای با افزایش فروش بیشتر از ۲۵٪`
- `کدام سهم‌ها فروششان دو برابر پارسال شده؟`
- `فروش کدام شرکت‌ها از میانگین دوازده‌ماهه بیشتر شده؟`

### Routing Safeguards

The following must not route to this feature:

- `رشد فروش کچاد چقدر است؟` → single-symbol metric/analysis path.
- `روند فروش کچاد را نشان بده` → monthly-sales trend path (`077`).
- `آخرین فروش کچاد چقدر است؟` → monthly-sales point/snapshot path (`069`/`070`).
- `ترکیب فروش محصولات کچاد` → product revenue mix (`075`).
- `لیست نمادهای با P/E زیر ۵` → generic scanner, without this specialization.
- `شرکت‌هایی که سود خالصشان رشد کرده` → net-profit growth scanner metric, not sales growth.

If both sales growth and other scanner conditions are requested, this feature contributes its validated sales-growth condition to the existing generic scanner plan rather than preventing composite scanning.

## Comparison Baseline Semantics

### 1. Previous Month

Canonical baseline:

`PreviousMonth`

Typical phrases:

- `نسبت به ماه قبل`
- `در مقایسه با ماه قبل`
- `ماه به ماه`
- `MoM`
- `نسبت به دوره قبل`, when the subject is explicitly monthly sales

Calculation facts:

- current value = latest eligible complete `MONTHLY_SALES` observation;
- baseline value = immediately preceding eligible monthly observation;
- canonical growth metric may reuse `MONTHLY_SALES_GROWTH_MOM` when its policy and evidence match this definition.

### 2. Same Month Previous Year

Canonical baseline:

`SameMonthPreviousYear`

Typical phrases:

- `نسبت به سال گذشته`
- `نسبت به پارسال`
- `نسبت به ماه مشابه سال قبل`
- `نسبت به دوره مشابه سال قبل`
- `سال به سال`
- `YoY`

For monthly-sales questions, `سال گذشته` and `دوره مشابه سال قبل` resolve to the same-month-previous-year baseline, not the immediately previous calendar year total.

Calculation facts:

- current value = latest eligible complete `MONTHLY_SALES` observation;
- baseline value = the matching month in the previous year;
- canonical growth metric may reuse `MONTHLY_SALES_GROWTH_YOY` when its policy and evidence match this definition.

### 3. Average of Previous 12 Months

Canonical baseline:

`AveragePrevious12Months`

Typical phrases:

- `نسبت به میانگین ۱۲ ماهه`
- `بالاتر از متوسط فروش یک‌سال گذشته`
- `در مقایسه با متوسط دوازده ماه اخیر`
- `vs 12-month average`

Calculation facts:

- current value = latest eligible complete monthly sales observation;
- baseline value = governed average of the previous 12 eligible monthly observations;
- the current month must not be included in its own baseline;
- the implementation may reuse `AVG_12M_MONTHLY_SALES` only when its documented window policy is equivalent; otherwise add a governed comparison metric/calculator rather than calculating in the renderer or LLM.

### Generic Growth Without an Explicit Baseline

For questions such as `لیست سهم‌هایی که رشد فروش داشته‌اند`, the product must execute rather than depend on a clarification whenever the configured policy permits it.

Default policy:

- baseline = `SameMonthPreviousYear`;
- threshold = `Positive`, equivalent to growth percent `> 0`;
- the response must disclose that the comparison with the same month of the previous year was inferred by product policy.

This default must be versioned and configurable in the semantic/scanner policy. It must not be hidden in prompts or UI code. If the policy is disabled, the system returns a focused clarification between `ماه قبل`, `ماه مشابه سال قبل`, and `میانگین ۱۲ ماهه`.

## Threshold Semantics

### Positive Growth

When no numeric threshold is supplied:

```text
CurrentSales > BaselineSales
GrowthPercent > 0
```

Equality is not growth.

### Percentage Threshold

Examples:

- `بیش از ۳۰ درصد رشد`
- `حداقل ۳۰٪ رشد`
- `رشد بالای ۳۰ درصد`

Canonical formula:

```text
GrowthPercent = ((CurrentSales - BaselineSales) / BaselineSales) * 100
```

Operator mapping:

- `بیشتر از`, `بالای`, `بیش از` → `>`
- `حداقل`, `کمتر نباشد از` → `>=`

### Multiple Threshold

Examples:

- `حداقل ۲ برابر شده`
- `بیش از ۱.۵ برابر میانگین ۱۲ ماهه`
- `فروششان دو برابر پارسال است`

Canonical formula:

```text
GrowthMultiple = CurrentSales / BaselineSales
```

Interpretation rule:

`۲ برابر` means the current sales value is at least/effectively `2.0 ×` the baseline, which is equivalent to `100%` growth. It must not be interpreted as `200%` growth unless the user explicitly says `۲۰۰ درصد رشد`.

The response should display both the multiple and equivalent percentage when useful, while preserving the user's requested threshold representation in the explanation.

### Invalid or Ambiguous Thresholds

- Negative thresholds are allowed only when the user explicitly asks for decline/tolerance semantics; that is outside this feature's default growth-only scope and should fall back to the generic scanner or clarification.
- `x برابر رشد` may be normalized to the current-to-baseline multiple policy above, with an explanation.
- Unsupported units or malformed values must not generate executable filters.

## Eligible Data and Period Policy

A result row is eligible only when:

- the canonical company and tradable symbol mapping are valid;
- the current monthly-sales observation is complete and eligible under the existing source-selection policy;
- the requested baseline can be resolved from eligible observations;
- both current and baseline values have explicit valid value states;
- baseline sales are greater than zero for percentage/multiple calculations;
- the source freshness policy does not classify the data as unusable.

Missing, null, invalid, or zero baseline values must not be converted into infinite growth. Those rows are excluded and counted in coverage metadata.

### Cross-Symbol Period Consistency

Default scanner policy should use a governed market-wide evaluation cutoff so rows are meaningfully comparable:

- determine the latest monthly period with acceptable market coverage according to a configurable minimum-coverage policy;
- evaluate all symbols against that target period where possible;
- do not silently compare each symbol using unrelated latest periods and present them as one homogeneous ranking.

If the product deliberately supports per-symbol latest periods, the period must be displayed per row and mixed-period status must be explicit. The preferred default for this feature is a common target period.

## Structured Scanner Plan

Suggested extension or specialization:

```json
{
  "intent": "SalesGrowthSymbolScanner",
  "universe": "AllEligibleEquities",
  "salesGrowth": {
    "currentSelector": "LatestCompleteCommonMonthlyPeriod",
    "comparisonBaseline": "SameMonthPreviousYear",
    "threshold": {
      "kind": "Percent",
      "operator": ">",
      "value": 30
    },
    "baselineOrigin": "Explicit",
    "thresholdOrigin": "Explicit"
  },
  "sort": [
    { "field": "GrowthPercent", "direction": "Descending" }
  ],
  "pagination": {
    "page": 1,
    "pageSize": 20
  }
}
```

The validated plan contains canonical semantic identifiers, comparison-policy version, metric-definition version, and origin metadata. It contains no SQL and no LLM-generated formula.

## Result Table Contract

The response uses the existing scanner structured-table contract and pagination.

### Mandatory Columns

In accordance with Feature `008`, the first columns remain:

1. `نماد`
2. `شرکت`

### Required Sales-Growth Columns

The default table for this feature contains:

3. `فروش آخرین دوره`
4. comparison value with a dynamic localized title:
   - `فروش ماه قبل`, or
   - `فروش ماه مشابه سال قبل`, or
   - `میانگین فروش ۱۲ ماهه`
5. `درصد رشد`
6. `نسبت فروش` (current ÷ comparison), when a multiple was requested or when enabled by the presentation policy

Example:

| نماد | شرکت | فروش آخرین دوره | فروش ماه مشابه سال قبل | درصد رشد | نسبت فروش |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| نمونه | شرکت نمونه | ۲٬۰۰۰ میلیارد ریال | ۱٬۰۰۰ میلیارد ریال | ۱۰۰٪ | ۲٫۰۰ برابر |

The result metadata must also include, even if not rendered as default columns:

- current period;
- baseline period or average window;
- value unit and scale;
- source/provider;
- observation/sync freshness;
- applied operator and threshold;
- explicit versus inferred comparison/threshold origin;
- exclusion/coverage counts;
- comparison-policy and metric-definition versions.

No market price, daily change, P/E, P/S, market cap, score, or unrelated metric is automatically added unless explicitly requested or used as another scanner condition, consistent with Feature `008`.

Internal/debug columns must never be displayed.

## Sorting, Pagination, and Empty Results

Default behavior:

- sort by `GrowthPercent` descending;
- stable tie-break by canonical symbol ID/symbol;
- use existing scanner pagination and maximum result limits;
- return the total matched count and evaluated coverage count.

When no symbols match, return a deterministic empty result explaining:

- the comparison baseline;
- threshold;
- target period;
- number of eligible/evaluated symbols;
- whether missing/stale baseline coverage reduced the universe.

The AI must not invent candidate symbols to avoid an empty table.

## Explainability

The response must state the interpreted query in concise Persian, for example:

`نمادهایی نمایش داده شده‌اند که فروش آخرین ماه کامل آن‌ها بیش از ۳۰٪ نسبت به ماه مشابه سال قبل رشد کرده است.`

When defaults are inferred:

`چون مبنای مقایسه مشخص نشده بود، رشد نسبت به ماه مشابه سال قبل در نظر گرفته شد.`

For each row, deterministic explain-why data must retain:

- current sales and period;
- baseline sales and period/window;
- formula inputs;
- calculated percentage and multiple;
- threshold match;
- source and freshness.

The LLM may summarize these facts but may not calculate or alter them.

## Provider and Calculation Rules

- Reuse canonical normalized monthly-sales data and existing source-selection policies.
- Prefer existing persisted derived metrics when their calculation policy exactly matches this feature.
- Do not synchronously call Noavaran, CyclicalWaves, CodalDB, or another external provider from the AI request/rendering path.
- If a required average comparison is not already governed, add it through the derived-metric/calculation foundation rather than inline query arithmetic duplicated across adapters.
- Avoid provider-specific intent aliases or result contracts.
- Preserve one canonical company/symbol identity across providers.

## Web and Telegram Behavior

### Web

- Render the structured paginated table.
- Show interpreted comparison, threshold, target period, unit, and freshness.
- Preserve the table through conversation reload using the versioned structured response contract.

### Telegram

- Reuse the Telegram AI adapter and compact table/list conventions.
- Include symbol, current sales, baseline sales, and growth percentage.
- Preserve ordering and pagination using replay-safe callbacks.
- Include a concise comparison-period/freshness footer.
- Do not send a chart unless a separate supported chart request is made.

## Security, Billing, and Operational Rules

- Reuse existing identity, entitlement, rate-limit, and Billing reservation/finalization flows.
- Failed parsing, unavailable evidence, or deterministic empty results must follow existing credit-consumption policy and must not create duplicate ledger entries.
- Emit telemetry for detected aliases, resolved baseline, threshold kind/operator/value, policy defaults, target period, eligible/evaluated/matched/excluded counts, latency, stale/partial status, and failures.
- User text must never be translated into arbitrary SQL or executable expressions.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The AI detects plural/list sales-growth discovery without requiring an exact predefined sentence.
2. Questions with `ماه قبل` resolve to `PreviousMonth`.
3. Monthly-sales questions with `سال گذشته`, `پارسال`, `دوره مشابه سال قبل`, or `ماه مشابه سال قبل` resolve to `SameMonthPreviousYear`.
4. Questions with `میانگین ۱۲ ماهه` resolve to `AveragePrevious12Months` and exclude the current month from the baseline window.
5. A generic `رشد فروش داشته‌اند` question uses the governed default comparison and `GrowthPercent > 0`, with inferred-default disclosure.
6. Percentage thresholds preserve the requested strict/inclusive operator.
7. Multiple thresholds use `CurrentSales / BaselineSales`; `۲ برابر` equals `2.0×` current-to-baseline and `100%` growth.
8. The LLM never calculates sales, percentages, multiples, periods, or result rows.
9. Rows with missing/invalid/non-positive baselines are excluded and never represented as infinite growth.
10. The scanner uses a governed common evaluation period by default, or explicitly discloses mixed periods if that policy is enabled.
11. The result table contains symbol, company, latest-period sales, requested comparison sales, growth percent, and optional/requested multiple.
12. The comparison column title changes according to the resolved baseline.
13. The table does not automatically add market-price or unrelated financial columns.
14. Results are sorted deterministically and support pagination.
15. Empty results are factual and include coverage/exclusion context without fabricated symbols.
16. Single-symbol sales questions, trends, product mix, and unrelated scanner questions keep their existing routing.
17. Web and Telegram use the same validated financial result and preserve ordering/values.
18. Every result exposes evidence, source freshness, periods, policy versions, and explicit/inferred interpretation metadata.
19. No synchronous external-provider call occurs in the AI query path.
20. Regression tests cover Persian spelling variants, Arabic/Persian character normalization, ZWNJ, `%`/`درصد`, Persian/Latin decimals, and `برابر` wording.
