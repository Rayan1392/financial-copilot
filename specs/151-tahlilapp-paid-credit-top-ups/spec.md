# Feature 151 — TahlilApp paid credit top-ups

**Status:** specification only. Independent of [Feature 152 Max](../152-tahlilapp-unlimited-subscription/spec.md). [Feature 139](../139-tahlilapp-session-exchange/TahlilAppIntegrationContract.md) owns identity and the pending shared full-window/one-time-Free lifecycle work (T-40–T-43). TahlilApp source is outside this repository.

## Verified implementation boundary

`TahlilAppLaunchUseCase.ExecuteAsync` exchanges a launch code, provisions by `(Provider, ExternalUserId)`, then synchronizes plans through `ExternalSubscriptionSynchronizer.SynchronizeAsync`. The official payload supplies `user_id` and `subscription.plans[]` entries with `slug`, `starts_at`, `expires_at`; it supplies **no separate subscription ID**. `TahlilAppSubscriptionPolicy.Decide` constructs an identity key from that tuple. See `src/backend/FinancialCopilot.Application/Authentication/TahlilApp/TahlilAppLaunchUseCase.cs`, `src/backend/FinancialCopilot.Domain/Identity/TahlilApp/TahlilAppSubscriptionPolicy.cs`, `src/backend/FinancialCopilot.Infrastructure/Authentication/TahlilApp/TahlilAppExchangeParser.cs`, and `src/backend/FinancialCopilot.Infrastructure/Billing/Persistence/ExternalSubscriptionSynchronizer.cs`.

Billing has a finite aggregate wallet, immutable ledger, unique grant keys, external plan windows, **currently 30-day allowance periods**, and source-attributed charge rows (`BillingPersistenceModels`, `BillingEntityConfigurations`, `SubscriptionCreditLifecycleService`). That monthly implementation **does not meet** the confirmed product rule: Free 250 once per user; Plus 260 and Pro 2600 once per entire authoritative subscription window, with no day-30/day-60 refill. Feature 139 T-40–T-43 own the shared correction. `UsageReservationAuthorizationService` holds only aggregate wallet credits; `UsageFinalizationService` attributes charges to allowance periods. `UsageRefundService` currently adds the **entire** usage refund to the wallet even when `SubscriptionCreditLifecycleService.ApplyRefundAsync` cannot restore an expired source. These paths require targeted extension for expiring top-ups. Telegram `BillingPurchaseUseCases.FulfillApproved` demonstrates transactional ledger/wallet grant, but `ProcessPaymentCallbackAsync` returns `NotConfigured`; no verified TahlilApp payment webhook is implemented. `UsageController.GetMyUsage` is the existing balance read.

## Confirmed rules and eligibility

- TahlilApp owns offers, prices, checkout, server-side payment verification and payment audit. SapioAI receives a positive integer `creditsPurchased` only after verification. Active quantities are **100, 500 and 1000**. A versioned allowlist checks the quantity; optional package ID/version must agree. SapioAI does not validate money or provider units.
- Commercial subscription context shown by TahlilApp: Plus 1,000,000, Pro 5,000,000 and Max 25,000,000 TOMAN per 90 days. These prices and the commercial duration do not enter SapioAI's top-up webhook or determine its expiry. TahlilApp's authoritative validity dates do. Free's validity is its separately assigned expiration, never an assumed 90 days.
- The purchaser is a linked, enabled individual account with an active, unexpired Plus or Pro TahlilApp subscription. Check before checkout using authoritative status; at grant, validate the **original paid Plus/Pro source** rather than requiring the current effective plan to remain Plus/Pro. Stale grace cannot authorize a new purchase. Free, active Max, expired, revoked or suspended accounts cannot start a new top-up. **Confirmed:** multiple purchases are allowed before or after zero balance. A verified Plus/Pro purchase made before transition to Max remains bound to its original window; see delayed-delivery rule below.
- Each purchase is bound to its **original authoritative TahlilApp subscription window** `(slug, starts_at, expires_at)`, not the plan active on delivery. The webhook repeats those existing official fields as the checkout-time snapshot. The tuple must match one persisted `ExternalPlanSubscriptionRow` for the linked account. TahlilApp retains it with the purchase. No new upstream subscription-ID field is required unless TahlilApp proves distinct windows can share an identical tuple. A renewal has a new tuple and cannot inherit the top-up. Feature 139 replaces 30-day allowance cycles with full-window sources before top-up fulfillment is enabled.
- Top-up adds exactly the purchased credits to the numeric wallet without changing tier or subscription dates or creating a fresh validity period. Unused purchased credits expire at the original window's `expires_at`, or are forfeited on authoritative revocation. Expiry removes only that source's remaining credits, with no monthly reset or renewal/upgrade/downgrade carry. If the original window is already over at webhook delivery, persist `expired-unfulfilled` without spendable grant and reconcile financial refund separately.
- **Recommended delayed-delivery rule:** if payment occurred during the original Plus/Pro window and Max became active before delivery, grant only while that original window is still active and unrevoked; keep the original expiry and source, even though Max usage itself makes no numeric debit. If that window ended, record `expired-unfulfilled`. This is distinct from a prohibited **new** Max checkout. It does not add Max credits or extend any subscription.

**Examples:** (A) A Plus user receives 260 credits for the entire authoritative subscription, spends all 260, then buys 100; the 100 remains usable only until that original expiry. (B) A Pro user has 80 remaining and buys 500; the wallet has 580 usable credits, with unchanged subscription expiry. (C) If 40 top-up credits remain at expiry, those 40 expire and never enter a renewal. (D) Day 30 and day 60 generate zero new subscription credits.

## Source attribution, consumption and concurrency

Extend Billing source accounting, not the wallet count. Persist a `TopUpCreditSource` per granted purchase linked to `ExternalPlanSubscriptionRow`, with granted/remaining credits, expiry, grant ledger ID and revision. Expiry is the external window expiry, not a calculated boundary. Feature 139 T-40 first changes allowance source rows to represent one full subscription window and one-time Free. Extend usage allocation to identify **either** an allowance source or a top-up source with exact grant ID/amount; do not overload the allowance-period FK. Add per-reservation source holds because current reservations store only an aggregate hold. Spendable balance and admission must exclude expired sources even before cleanup, while preserving unrelated finite balances.

Admission holds finite credits from all expiring sources by earliest expiry, then source start/grant time, then stable source key; allowance sources win an exact tie. Other general wallet credits follow. Store each hold with reservation/source in the same transaction as aggregate `ReservedAmount`. Capacity upgrade extends holds under the **original finite billing mode**. Finalization consumes held sources and writes charge allocations, wallet debit and reservation status atomically. Release/cancellation returns still-valid held credits to their source; after expiry/revocation, it expires/forfeits them instead. A reservation admitted before natural expiry may finish under its original finite sources after expiry up to its hold; unheld remainder expires at the boundary. Abandoned-reservation cleanup releases or expires holds before source closure. Revocation follows Feature 139's release-pending-reservations policy. Use database constraints/revisions and account/wallet serialization for grant, hold, settlement, expiry and refund; an aggregate balance check cannot identify expirable credits.

Usage refunds restore only the portion attributed to each still-active source, capped by the original allocation and earlier refunds. The portion funded by an expired/revoked top-up is recorded as refunded usage **without increasing spendable wallet balance**. Write an offsetting expired-credit adjustment linked to that refund in the same transaction so ledger-derived wallet balance remains correct. This requires a targeted `UsageRefundService.RefundAsync` change (it currently adds the whole amount); no expired source is revived. An active top-up refund restores its original source, never a renewal source. Payment refunds/chargebacks are separate: record reversal and prevent regrant; commercial clawback after consumption remains a Product decision.

## Atomic delivery and recovery

Persist purchase and event receipts with unique `(partner,purchaseId)`, `(partner,paymentId)`, `(partner,eventId)` keys and a unique ledger grant key. Immutable duplicate comparison covers user, original window tuple, quantity, optional package and payment ID. **First check for a persisted purchase/payment/event outcome and return an identical result, even after expiry or plan change; only a new purchase undergoes current eligibility and source-window checks.** Another event ID for the same payment returns the stored result; conflicting reuse returns 409. After authoritative status refresh for a new purchase, resolve the **original** window and serialize grant against expiry/reservation changes. Purchase row, top-up source, positive ledger adjustment, wallet increment, audit/outbox and `granted` outcome commit in one Billing transaction. On uniqueness/concurrency race, roll back and reread the winner. A timeout after commit returns its persisted result. Persist `expired-unfulfilled` or `ineligible-unfulfilled` for a verified payment that cannot be applied, without wallet/ledger credit grant. TahlilApp retains paid-but-ungranted purchases, retries transient failures with stable IDs and reconciles against SapioAI records. Browser redirect never authorizes a grant.

## User journey and rollout

TahlilApp checks identity/eligibility, captures the original window tuple, sells an allowed quantity, verifies payment and durably queues authenticated delivery. SapioAI grants once and exposes self-scoped purchase status. Early browser return shows **top-up processing**; only committed `granted` triggers refresh through `GET /api/v1/usage/me`. Delivery never depends on browser return. Deploy schema/secret with webhook disabled, run PostgreSQL grant/expiry/reservation/refund race tests and joint staging delivery, then enable offers. Monitor paid-but-ungranted, expired-unfulfilled and conflicts. Preserve Feature 139 staging gates.

## Remaining product decisions

Set the refund/hold SLA for a verified payment whose original window expired or whose account became ineligible before delivery. Decide payment chargeback clawback after consumption; until then hold for manual reconciliation without inventing debt or deleting history. TahlilApp retains pricing and payment audit responsibility. The precise Free assigned-expiration source is the shared Feature 139 contract detail still requiring confirmation; it does not change top-up delivery.
